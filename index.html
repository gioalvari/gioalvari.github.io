Background

Education & research

Formal training, research experience and the publication behind my approach to evaluation.

Experimental design still shapes how I evaluate models and production systems.

I started in biology, not software. My master's was in quantitative and computational biology at the University of Trento, followed by research at DKFZ in Heidelberg and collaboration with EMBL on single-cell eQTL mapping.

That background still shapes how I work. In biology, the difference between an interesting result and a reliable one often comes down to how the test was set up.

I then moved into industry: applied machine learning on customer data, forecasting systems, and more recently LLM-based tools for operations. I live in Florence; Italian is my native language, I use English at C1 level and Spanish at B1 level.
