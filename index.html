Experiment 01 / Forecasting

GridCast ↗

A leakage-safe workbench for probabilistic energy forecasting on public PJM load and ERA5 weather.

Question
How should uncertainty travel with a weekly load forecast?
Method
Chronological validation, explicit baselines and conformal calibration.
Limit
Foundation-model results are isolated where pretraining overlap cannot be ruled out.

LocalLLM Bench ↗

A reproducible harness that evaluates local language models on quality, speed and memory together.

Question

Method
Seeded load, binary fingerprints and run-level confidence intervals.
Limit

Background

How I got here

From computational biology to applied machine-learning systems.

A result that looks good is not necessarily a result that is true.

I started in biology, not software. My master's was in computational biology at the University of Trento, and I spent a year at DKFZ and EMBL in Heidelberg working on single-cell eQTL mapping. That work became a paper in Genome Biology; the containerised pipeline is still on my first GitHub account.

That background still shapes how I work. In biology, the difference between an interesting result and a reliable one often comes down to how the test was set up.

I then moved into industry: applied machine learning on customer data, forecasting systems, and more recently LLM-based tools for operations. I live in Florence and work in Italian, English and Spanish.
